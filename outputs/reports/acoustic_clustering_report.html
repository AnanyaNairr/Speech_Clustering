7. Critical Limitations & Responsible Use

1. Dataset Demographics & Volunteer Bias: Google Speech Commands consists primarily of healthy adult volunteers speaking clear, isolated words in controlled conditions. Children, elderly users, non-native accents, and distinct regional dialects are under-represented.

2. Exclusion of Dysarthric & Impaired Speech: Individuals with motor disabilities often present dysarthric speech, vocal tremor, reduced breath support, or atypical articulation—the exact population needing hands-free accessibility. These clusters DO NOT reflect dysarthric acoustic patterns.

3. Exploratory Disclaimer: These unsupervised clusters represent mathematical acoustic proximities from a single sample. They must never be treated as immutable phonetic truths. Any production accessibility control interface must conduct empirical user testing and offer user-calibrated command customization.
